- [x] 2. WebAudio sound module (`src/ui/sound.ts`)
  - Implement `createSound(deps, masterGain?)` as in design.md, with the structural `AudioContextLike` types, `MASTER_GAIN_MAX = 0.25` and `MAX_CLACKS_PER_SECOND = 40`.   - Write `src/ui/sound.prop.test.ts` with fast-check for (d): P-d1 nothing before unlock; P-d2 muted starts nothing; P-d3 master at most 0.25 and effective gain at most 0.25 on the fake graph, even when a larger master value is passed; P-d4 spaces and newlines make no clack; P-d5 never more than 40 clacks in any 1000 ms window, and acceptance resumes after the window; P-d6 no throw for a missing, undefined or throwing factory; P-d7 repeated `unlock` makes one context.
  - Add example tests for the clack filter (band-pass, 1800 to 2500 Hz, Q 1.2), the thunk (low-pass, 220 Hz) and the bell (three sine partials at 1320, 3168 and 5148 Hz).
